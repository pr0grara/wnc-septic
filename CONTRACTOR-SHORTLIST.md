# Western NC Septic: anchor contractor shortlist

Researched 2026-09-22 (tunnel thread 808, one web-research agent, 112 page opens). Site:
westernncseptic.com, tracking number (828) 574-6377. At research time the site had 13 unique
people all-time and 7 in the trailing 28 days with nobody answering. Goal: ONE Henderson or
Buncombe county pumper who also holds an inspector card, on a flat monthly rent-the-site retainer.

Lead pattern to sell against: pre-purchase inspections in Arden and Hendersonville (realtors and
buyers, one lender-required well-to-septic setback letter), routine pumping, one full tank near
Hendersonville, one grinder-pump repair, one caller west of the footprint (Franklin to Bryson
City). The buyer needs a pump truck AND a certified inspector, based within about 30 minutes of
Hendersonville and Arden, with room on the calendar.

Every phone number below came from a page the agent opened (linked). "Unconfirmed" means it is not
stated anywhere public, not that it is false. Nothing here has been verified by a human yet.

## Licensing lookups (verify by hand before promising a lead type)

- **NCOWCICB (installers and inspectors):** [certification list page](https://ncowcicb.info/certification-list/), with the [roster by county (9-16-26 PDF)](https://ncowcicb.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/9-16-26-NCOWCICB-CERTIFIED-LIST-BY-COUNTY.pdf), the [roster by last name](https://ncowcicb.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/9-16-26-NCOWCICB-CERTIFIED-LIST-BY-LAST-NAME.pdf), and the [PCI inspector list](https://ncowcicb.info/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/4-6-26-NCOWCICB-PCI-CERTIFIED-LIST.pdf). Board phone 336-202-3126. The PDFs would not render from the tunnel, so verification relied on search-indexed roster text: confirmed Chad McCoy (North Buncombe), Lusk Septic & Repair (Mitchell, Mason), Mike Lusk Jr. Not surfaced: Lopez, Metcalf (Aiden and Jeremy), Prickett, Laughter, Holden. Open the county PDF on the phone and search those names before quoting inspector status.
- **NC DEQ septage management firm permit (pumpers):** [DEQ septage program](https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/waste-management/solid-waste-section/special-wastes-and-alternative-handling/septage) links "Find a Permitted Firm" at a [Laserfiche document](https://edocs.deq.nc.gov/WasteManagement/DocView.aspx?id=2294274&dbid=0&repo=WasteManagement) and a [firm records folder](https://edocs.deq.nc.gov/WasteManagement/Browse.aspx?id=802&dbid=0&repo=WasteManagement). Both returned a "cookies must be enabled" shell from the tunnel, so no pumper permits were confirmed; a normal browser should open them.
